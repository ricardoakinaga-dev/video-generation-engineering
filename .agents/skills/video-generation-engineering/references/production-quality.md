# Production quality and Triple-AAA evidence

This reference owns the boundary between a coherent production plan and observed audiovisual quality. It is intentionally separate from `vge_core.py`: planning is canonical authored/derived state, while quality is immutable evidence attached to collected artifacts.

## Three independent gates

Architecture asks whether ownership, dependencies, contracts, progressive disclosure, safety boundaries and traceability are coherent. Verification asks whether deterministic software, fixtures, adapters, runtime contracts and distribution checks pass. Production asks whether exact generated bytes were collected, decoded, inspected and accepted for the claimed scope. A PASS in one gate cannot repair a gap in another.

## Status vocabulary

Use `PASS`, `FAIL`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `NOT_OBSERVED`, `NOT_RUN`, `UNKNOWN` and `BLOCKED` deliberately. The six release-facing quality states are PASS/FAIL/PARTIAL/NOT_OBSERVED/NOT_APPLICABLE/BLOCKED; NOT_RUN and UNKNOWN remain explicit internal non-evidentiary states. `PASS` requires an explicit oracle strong enough for the claim, evidence bound to the exact artifact hash, confidence that is not `UNKNOWN`, and limitations. Node presence, a prompt, a workflow schema, a contact sheet or ffprobe metadata never proves semantics.

Oracle strength is claim-specific: frame checks can support visible snapshots, sequence or multi-frame checks are required for temporal/lip-sync claims, audio checks are required for sound claims, transition checks are required for adjacent-shot claims, and human checks are required for editorial judgement. A weaker oracle cannot be promoted by metadata or by an aggregate status.

## Long-form ladder

The minimum production ladder is:

- `LF-001`: 10–15 seconds, one physical interaction with a measurable start state, contact phases and result state.
- `LF-002`: 20–30 seconds, dialogue plus interaction; semantics, voice, performance, lip-sync and mix are separate channels.
- `LF-003`: 45–60 seconds, demanding bounded production with multiple dependent shots, scene Bible, shot graph, continuity state, audio timeline, repair budget and human checkpoint.
- `LF-004` (optional): 90–120 seconds, with branches, recovery checkpoints, provenance manifest and explicit editorial review.

Each case retains intent, plan, Scene Bible, shot graph, continuity ledger, prompts, profile, workflow fingerprint, immutable attempts, artifacts, observations, transition reports, repair decisions and assembly manifest. A production `PASS` additionally requires the case-specific contract: LF-001 must bind observed contact phases and vehicle state; LF-002 must bind observed dialogue and audio timelines; LF-003 must bind all Scene Bible identity/continuity fields, at least two identities, five timed narrative beats, 6–12 dependent shots, multiple camera signatures, dialogue/contact/audio bindings, a demonstrated repair or hash-bound `NO_NATURAL_REPAIR_CASE`, and final media duration within the 45–60 second target. Production attempts must come from the trusted ComfyUI submit/collect path with `PRODUCTION` evidence scope; fixture, synthetic, placeholder and copied-short-media records are rejected. If a provider, model or media oracle is unavailable, the case remains `NOT_RUN`, `UNKNOWN` or `BLOCKED`.

## Continuity scorecard

Every adjacent-shot review scores these dimensions independently: identity, wardrobe, hair, object state/ownership, vehicle, environment, lighting, screen direction, camera geography, gaze, emotional continuity, dialogue, temporal continuity and audio. A required dimension must have an oracle and evidence; observed evidence carries exact content hashes, and any observed PASS/FAIL/PARTIAL scorecard must bind to existing artifact bytes. The aggregate is FAIL before PASS; mixed PASS and unobserved is PARTIAL.

## Contracts

- Shot acceptance binds canonical shot revision, immutable attempt and exact artifact hash. Generation acceptance and editorial acceptance are separate.
- Transition acceptance binds existing previous and next artifact bytes, previous artifact end state to next shot start state, a continuity scorecard and an explicit hash-bound side-by-side comparison across every continuity dimension. Long-form PASS additionally validates the canonical shot order, every adjacent transition, immutable observation IDs, strict assembly manifest, current final media QA and a separate human editorial acceptance record. The smallest repair owner is selected from reference conditioning, continuity, cinematography, adapter, decomposition, dialogue, audio, performance or human review.
- Re-anchor actions are `CONTINUE`, `RE_ANCHOR`, `RESET`, `REGENERATE` and `SPLIT`. Drift is observed evidence, not a prompt instruction.
- First/last-frame support is feature-scoped. A capability probe must inspect endpoint identity, motion path, object state and delivered artifact independently for `FIRST_ONLY`, `LAST_ONLY` and `FIRST_AND_LAST`; node metadata alone is not proof. Observed PASS contracts also carry the successful capability-probe attempt, complete model identity/hash, shot-contract and generated-artifact provenance envelope, with output bytes distinct from every required input.
- Physics is represented as observable assertions across `APPROACH`, `PRE_CONTACT`, `CONTACT`, `FORCE_OR_ARTICULATION`, `TRANSFER_OR_MOTION`, `RELEASE` and `RESULT`.
- Dialogue semantics, voice, performance, lip-sync and mix are independent observations. Audio layers have their own time intervals, priority, mix role and causal source.
- Repair is bounded by regeneration, attempt, runtime, cost and human-review budgets; unaffected siblings are preserved. A demonstrated lineage must identify diagnosis, owner, bounded delta, failed/new parent-linked attempts and artifacts, chronological before/after semantic observations showing the failed dimension improve, and every affected transition revalidated. Validation remains `PARTIAL` until an authorized execution ledger with measured usage, hash-bound evidence and successful-attempt/artifact provenance is attached.

## Commands

The CLI exposes these contracts without requiring a provider:

```bash
python3 scripts/vge.py media-qa artifact.mp4
python3 scripts/vge.py scorecard scorecard.json
python3 scripts/vge.py transition transition.json
python3 scripts/vge.py reanchor drift.json --shot-id shot_002
python3 scripts/vge.py repair-plan repair.json
python3 scripts/vge.py semantic observation.json
```

These commands validate reports; they do not silently generate media, upload references or approve a final delivery.
