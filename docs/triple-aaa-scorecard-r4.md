## Maturity

The R4 maturity report is expected to remain Level 3 (`RUNTIME_PROVENANCE`) because structural, deterministic and runtime-provenance gates pass, while the full audiovisual, bounded-production and independent-critic gates do not all pass. Level 4/5 would require accepted audiovisual and bounded production evidence, not merely plans or mechanical metadata.

## Score interpretation

- `90–100`: strong evidence for the named scope, still bounded by its oracle.
- `70–89`: useful and implemented, but with material partial evidence.
- `40–69`: meaningful foundation with a production blocker.
- `<40`: unproven or blocked for the named capability.

The scorecard is subordinate to the capability matrix and the final closure report; it does not convert `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED` or `NOT_RUN` into `PROVEN`.
