# ADR-012 — Separate production evidence from canonical planning

Status: Accepted for Triple-AAA closure R2

## Context

The package had strong structural planning and runtime provenance, but a plan, workflow schema, contact sheet or ffprobe report could still be mistaken for audiovisual acceptance. The user-supplied production requirements require continuity, dialogue, audio, physics, semantic observation, bounded repair and long-form release evidence without weakening the existing plan-only boundary.

## Decision

Keep `vge_core.py` as the single owner of canonical authored/derived plan state. Add `vge_quality.py` as the single owner of category-separated quality contracts and decision records. Keep mechanical media heuristics in `vge_media.py`, runtime identity/fingerprints in `vge_runtime.py`, and provider authorization in `vge_provider.py`. Production evidence remains outside the installable Skill payload and is bound to exact artifact bytes.

Quality status cannot promote a plan lifecycle, and runtime discovery cannot promote semantic quality. A feature is scoped by model/runtime/workflow/profile fingerprint and expires on critical identity or behavioral changes.

## Consequences

- The package has a small additional module and a larger test surface, but no duplicated canonical state model.
- A final assembly may be mechanically valid while semantic/editorial acceptance is `NOT_RUN`.
- Full Triple-AAA release remains impossible when the local provider, second model, semantic oracle or human checkpoint is unavailable; the final report must say so.
- Historical `.gauntlet/` and verification records remain immutable and cannot be reused as current release proof.

## Rejected alternatives

1. Put semantic checks in `vge_core.validate`: rejected because structural plan validation and artifact observation have different lifecycles and evidence.
2. Treat prompt density or node presence as a quality oracle: rejected because neither observes generated bytes.
3. Auto-retry or silently fall back to a different model: rejected by the explicit authorization, provenance and no-blind-retry boundary.
